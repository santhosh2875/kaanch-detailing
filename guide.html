Checking folder

Ten stills, no video

The site wipes from one picture to the next as you scroll, so it needs five stills of the car and five of the bike, and nothing else. Each card turns green by itself when its file is in assets/stages.

These fill into the prompts below. The room is dark, so a rich colour such as green, red or blue shows up better than black.

The one rule that matters: the five pictures of a vehicle must line up exactly. Same vehicle, same camera angle, same room. If a version comes back with the vehicle moved, resized or redesigned, regenerate it. Small differences in reflections are fine.

1 The dirty car

Text to image. This is the master: every other car picture is an edit of it, so retry until you like the car and the room.

2 The same car in four more states

Image to image. Upload car-1-dirty as the reference every time. The prompts only change the surface of the car.

3 The dirty bike

Text to image, the same way as the car. To get the same room, you can also add car-1-dirty as a reference; the prompt then asks for that room with a motorcycle in place of the car.

4 The same bike in four more states

Image to image. Upload bike-1-dirty as the reference every time.

5 Put the files in the site

Save every download into assets/stages with exactly these names. JPG, PNG or WebP all work. Then open the website and reload.
